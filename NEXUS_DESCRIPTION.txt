[center][size=6][color=#f2cc66][b]END TURN PREVIEW[/b][/color][/size]
[i]See how your turn ends before you press End Turn.[/i][/center]

[line]

End Turn Preview simulates the rest of your turn with the game's own combat logic and shows the verified result on your HP and Block bars, pets included.

[size=4][color=#f2cc66][b]Features[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*]Projected [color=#e06666][b]HP[/b][/color] and [color=#6fa8dc][b]Block[/b][/color] on the native health bars, including enemy actions, poison, healing and Block expiration.[/*]
[*]A [color=#b48ee0]purple[/color] range when the result depends on hidden randomness or a choice.[/*]
[*]Coop: every player's bar shows its own forecast.[/*]
[*]Show it on End Turn hover, always, or while holding a shortcut.[/*]
[*]No save changes. Unverifiable results are hidden, never guessed.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][color=#f2cc66][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]BaseLib 3.4.8[/b]+ and the [b].NET 9 Runtime[/b].[/*]
[*]Extract into [b]Slay the Spire 2/mods/[/b] so you get [b]mods/EndTurnDamagePreview/[/b] (with its [b]simulator[/b] subfolder).[/*]
[*]Settings: [b]Settings > Mod Configuration > EndTurnDamagePreview[/b].[/*]
[/list]
